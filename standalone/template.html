05 · WHAT IT REFUSES

Designed refusals

  • Missing field: a blank title, date, medium, place or credit line stops the draft.
  • Unknown number: a catalogue number outside the corpus is refused, not approximated.
  • Out of scope: value, authenticity, insurance and rights questions are declined.
  • Source policy: a corpus whose source forbids it never reaches the model at all.
