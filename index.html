Exhibition labels, drafted one record at a time

A label draft you can check sentence by sentence.

Pick a catalogue record and Label Desk writes a short wall-label draft. Every sentence restates one field of the record, and the panel on the right reads each sentence back against that record. When the record cannot support a sentence, the tool says so instead of guessing. A draft is a starting point: a curator still reads it before it goes anywhere.

When it says no

Refusals are deliberate

  • A blank field. A record missing a title, date, medium, place or credit line is not drafted. The gap is reported, not filled in from elsewhere.
  • An unknown number. A catalogue number outside the corpus is refused, not approximated with a look-alike object.
  • A question out of scope. Value, authenticity, insurance and rights questions are declined; the tool holds no data to answer them.
  • A source that opts out. A record whose registered source forbids model input never reaches the model, whatever the page asks for.
